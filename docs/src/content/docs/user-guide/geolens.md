**Geolens** is a map-based module that helps project heads decide where and how to sample. It shows which surveillance sites are positive and lets you look at the environment around them, such as wetlands, poultry farms and bird activity, so you can plan better. Open it from **Monitor → Geolens** in the sidebar.

## The Map

The map shows your surveillance sites. Use the **Filters** button to narrow them by **Project**, **State**, **District**, **Disease**, **Site**, **Sample Type**, **Result** and **Wetland**. Click **Reset** to clear the filters.

Use the search box at the top to find a location by name.

## Layers & Analysis

The sidebar (**Layers & Analysis**) controls what is drawn on the map. Use the arrow at its edge to collapse or open it, and **Reset Map** to return to the default view.

| Section | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Base Maps** | OpenStreetMap, Satellite (Hybrid), Clean / Light Map, Topographic |
| **Layers** | **Surveillance Sites** |
| **Analysis Tools** | **Buffer & Proximity** |
| **Reference Layers** | **Wetlands**, **Poultry Farms**, **Heatmap (Bird Density)** |
| **Observations** | **eBird Observations**, shown as High, Medium and Low density |

### Proximity Analysis

Turn on **Buffer & Proximity**, pick a location (a site, a point you clicked, or a searched place) and set the distance with the radius slider (in km). The map then shows the extra layers, such as wetlands and poultry farms, that fall within that radius.

## Exploring Any Location

Click a point on the map, or search for a place, to open **Location Details** for it. It shows:

- **Current weather**: rain, dew point, humidity, wind, pressure, visibility and rainfall
- **Bird observations**: total birds and total species recorded
- **Poultry farms** nearby
- **Nearest wetland** and the shortest distance to it, with a link to **Get Directions on Google Maps**

## Comparing Two Sites

To decide between two places to sample, compare them side by side.

1. Click **Compare Sites** at the top of the map, or click a location and choose **Set as Site A** or **Set as Site B**.
2. Pick **Site A** and **Site B**. **Map Click Assignment** lets you choose whether your next click on the map sets Site A or Site B.
3. Read the **Site Comparison** panel. For each site it shows the **bird density**, **species count**, **total bird count**, **nearest wetland**, nearby poultry farms and current weather.
4. Click **View Detailed Comparison Report** to download a fuller report.

:::note
Bird observation and weather information come from external data sources. If a site shows "No bird data recorded" or "Weather unavailable", there is no data for that place right now.
:::
