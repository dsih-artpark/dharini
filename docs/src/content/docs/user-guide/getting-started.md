This page covers what every web user needs first: creating an account and workspace, logging in, and finding your way around. The role-specific guides pick up from here.

## Creating an Account

Anyone can create a new workspace from the Dharini landing page.

1. Open the Dharini web platform and click **Get Started**. The **Create your account** dialog opens.
2. Enter your **First name**, **Last name** and **Organisation name**.
3. Check the **Workspace name**. It is filled in from your organisation name, and you can change it. Use your organisation, a client, or a programme name.
4. Choose **Email** or **Phone**, enter it, and click **Send OTP**.
5. Enter the 6-digit code you receive and click **Verify & Create Workspace**. Your workspace is created and you are signed in.

:::note
The OTP is valid for **3 minutes**. If it expires, request a new one with **Resend code**, which becomes available after a 60-second countdown.
:::

The person who creates a workspace becomes its **workspace admin**.

## Logging In

1. Click **Sign in** on the landing page.
2. Choose **Email** or **Phone**, enter it, and click **Send OTP**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code and click **Verify & Login**.

If you belong to more than one workspace, you are asked to **Select a workspace** after logging in. You can switch workspace later from the menu at the top of the sidebar. The same menu has a **New Workspace** option.

You can only log in if you were added to a workspace or project, or if you created a workspace yourself. If you see "You don't belong to any workspace yet", ask your administrator to invite you.

### Accepting an Invitation

Administrators add people by invitation. When you open an invitation link:

- **Workspace invitation:** a **Welcome to your new workspace** screen asks for your **First name** and **Last name**. Enter them and continue.
- **Project invitation:** the link accepts the invite and opens a project set-up checklist. See [Setting Up a Project After an Invitation](/user-guide/team-admin#setting-up-a-project-after-an-invitation).

If the link says **Invalid Token**, it has expired or was already used. Ask the person who invited you to send a new one.

## Finding Your Way Around

After logging in, the sidebar has two groups. You only see the items your role allows.

**Monitor**

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | This month's overview, diseases and site details. See [Dashboards & Analysis](/user-guide/dashboard). |
| **Dashboard** | The disease surveillance dashboard. See [Dashboards & Analysis](/user-guide/dashboard). |
| **Geolens** | Map-based planning with environmental layers. See [GeoLens](/user-guide/geolens). |
| **Comparison** | Compare projects, sites or sample types. See [Dashboards & Analysis](/user-guide/dashboard). |
| **Lab Processing** | Receive, process and report on samples. See [Lab Technician Guide](/user-guide/lab-technician). |
| **SNP Analysis** | Submit sequences and review variants. See [SNP Analysis](/user-guide/snp-analysis). |

**Manage**

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Projects** | Create projects, sites and teams. See [Team Admin Guide](/user-guide/team-admin). |
| **Upload Data Files** | Import collection and lab data from spreadsheets. See [Team Admin Guide](/user-guide/team-admin#importing-existing-data). |
| **Team & User Management** | Add users and change their roles. |
| **Settings** | Workspace members, settings and other administration. |

The sidebar can be collapsed to icons. Your name and role appear at the bottom. Select them, then **View Profile** to see your details, your roles and your assigned projects, and to **Edit Profile** (name, phone number and photo). **Settings** and **Log out** are in the same menu.

## Roles

Dharini has five roles. Your role decides which menu items and actions you see.

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Team Admin** | Oversees a project and its teams. Follows the project's teams, sites, collection plans, visits, samples and test results, tracks progress on the Monitor pages (Dashboard, Comparison, GeoLens), and submits sequences for SNP Analysis. |
| **Collector Admin** | Lead field collector. Does everything a Collector can, and also adds, edits and removes the members of their collector team. |
| **Collector** | Collects samples in the field using the mobile app. Creates and updates sites, visits and samples, and can view the project's other data. |
| **Lab Admin** | Lead of a laboratory team. Does everything a Lab Member can, and also adds, edits and removes the members of their lab team. |
| **Lab Member** | Works in the laboratory using **Lab Processing**: receives samples, records pre-processing, extraction and testing, and updates test results. |

You assign one of these roles to each person when you add them to a project or team. See [Managing Team Access](/user-guide/team-admin#managing-team-access).

:::note
The person who creates a workspace automatically has full control of it (projects, teams, users, sample types, species and data). This comes with creating the workspace and is not one of the five roles you assign. Platform-wide super admins are managed by the Dharini team.
:::
