# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Dharini community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Dharini brings together researchers, government agencies, laboratories, developers, and field teams working on One Health disease surveillance. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community, and to treat the health, location, and personal data entrusted to this platform with the care it demands.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Collaborating respectfully across disciplines, organizations, and roles, from field collectors to lab technicians to maintainers
- Being patient with newcomers and with contributors whose expertise differs from our own
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback in code review, focused on the work rather than the person
- Disagreeing on technical matters respectfully, with reasoning and evidence
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Handling sensitive data only through approved channels, and redacting personal details from issues, logs, and screenshots
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the public health goals the project serves

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats of any kind, in public or private
- Discriminatory, sexualized, or derogatory language, imagery, or conduct
- Trolling, insulting or dismissive comments, and personal or political attacks
- Doxxing: publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address or phone number, without their explicit permission
- Sharing sensitive health, location, personal, surveillance, or field-collected data outside approved channels, including in issues, pull requests, discussions, test fixtures, or screenshots
- Posting credentials, OTPs, API keys, tokens, secrets, or production data in any project space
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate. Where sensitive data or secrets have been exposed, community leaders may remove the content immediately and coordinate any necessary credential rotation or data-protection response.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and project communication channels. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces, such as using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at **[dharini-support@artpark.in](mailto:dharini-support@artpark.in)**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

If you discover exposed sensitive data or credentials, please report it privately to the same address rather than drawing public attention to it.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions, including careless handling of sensitive data or secrets.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior or repeated mishandling of sensitive data.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals, or deliberate disclosure of sensitive health, personal, or surveillance data.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.
